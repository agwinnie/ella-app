ella Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 19, 2026

ella is a language learning app for practicing noun genders in Spanish, Italian, French, and Portuguese. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

Data We Collect

ella does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. All app data is stored locally on your device and never leaves it. This includes:

  • Your saved nouns and practice preferences
  • Your selected language and display settings
  • Your practice session history and card usage counter

Purchases and Subscriptions

ella offers an optional Passport subscription. All purchases are processed securely by Apple through the App Store. We do not have access to your payment information. For details on how Apple handles purchase data, please review Apple's Privacy Policy.

Third-Party Services

ella uses EmailJS solely to deliver feedback messages you choose to send from within the app. No personal data is collected or stored through this service beyond the content of the message you write.

Children's Privacy

ella does not knowingly collect any data from children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at ella.app.feedback@gmail.com.
